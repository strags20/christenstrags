What I see in most programs

5 things that are quietly holding your athletes back

7 seasons training Olympians and All-Stars taught me what actually moves the needle for female athletes. Here are 5 things I see in girls basketball strength programs that need to change:

01
Skipping the injury prevention work that actually matters for female athletes
Girls tear their ACL at 2 to 8 times the rate of male athletes in the same sport. That’s not bad luck — it’s anatomy. And most programs aren’t addressing it at all.
02
Conditioning that doesn’t match what the game actually demands
Too much focus on straight sprinting and mile times. Basketball is short explosive efforts with incomplete recovery — your conditioning should mirror that.
03
No structured lifting in-season
Two days per week minimum — even in-season. The athletes who maintain strength through a long season are the ones who finish strong in February when everyone else is running on empty.
04
Treating recovery as optional
A 2% drop in hydration causes a 10% drop in performance. Sleep drives adaptation. Your athletes are probably showing up to practice already behind — and nobody has told them why.
05
Running every player through the same program
Your point guard and your post player don’t play the same game. They shouldn’t train identically. You’re already thinking position-specific on the floor — this guide helps you extend that into the weight room.
